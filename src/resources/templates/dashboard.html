Welcome to Smart Library

Four quick steps to lend your first book:

  1. Register a member, then press Scan card with reader and tap their NFC card to link it.
  2. Register a book. Every physical copy gets its own RFID tag: press Scan tag with reader and tap it.
  3. Check the Reader badge at the top right. Green means the Arduino is connected.
  4. Open the RFID Counter: tap the member's card, then the book to borrow. Tap a borrowed book with no member selected to return it.
